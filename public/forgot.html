STEP 1 OF 4

Start with your email

Use the email connected to your student account. We’ll send instructions for the recovery options available to you.

Enter the address you use for this portal, not your student number.

Requesting an email will not change your password.

Have these readyAccess to your account email and the phone you previously verified. The reset link lasts 15 minutes.
New account or no verified phone?

Your email will explain the right next step. New accounts use their invitation and temporary password. If you haven’t verified a phone, you may need your saved backup code and authenticator instead.

Check your email

If an account matches the address below, we’ll email its next steps. For your privacy, we don’t confirm whether an account exists.

ADDRESS YOU ENTERED
  1. 1 Look for the recovery messageCheck your inbox and Spam or Junk folder. Delivery can take a few minutes.
  2. 2 Follow the steps in that emailA reset link lasts 15 minutes. You’ll then verify your phone code and choose a new password. If your account needs setup or another method, the email explains it.

Still can’t find the message?

Check the address above for spelling mistakes. Search your mail for “requested password reset” or “account recovery options”. If you requested more than one message, use the most recent one.

A delivered email may be filtered by your email provider. Sending repeatedly won’t guarantee inbox placement, and requests are limited for your safety.
