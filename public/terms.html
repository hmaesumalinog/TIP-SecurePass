Terms of use

Emails, text messages, and authenticator apps show this portal as Reset Workflow.

Academic demonstration only

This project studies password-recovery security and usability. It is not the official T.I.P. portal and does not provide enrollment, grades, or other official school services.

Use your assigned account

Use only the demonstration account assigned to you. Do not sign in to another person's account, share access, or submit someone else's contact details. Never reuse your official school password here.

Secure your account

Replace the temporary password at first sign-in, connect an authenticator, and privately save your backup codes. A mobile number is optional; only add a number you control and confirm its SMS code. Do not give administrators your password, OTP, authenticator setup key, or backup codes.

Test responsibly

Use the portal only for authorized demonstrations. Do not spam recovery requests, test accounts without permission, submit sensitive identity documents, or attempt to bypass access controls. Administrators may deactivate accounts to protect the demonstration.

Understand the limitations

Email and SMS delivery depend on external providers and cannot be guaranteed. Recovery links and codes expire. If you lose all enrolled methods, ask the project administrator for assistance. A request does not automatically establish ownership or restore access.

Your choice and support

If you do not agree, sign out and contact the project administrator who invited you. This is not an official school support channel. Contact that administrator for corrections, to stop participating, or to report account misuse.

Acknowledgment

The portal records the terms version and acceptance time. Changes may require another acknowledgment. These demonstration terms are not a substitute for institution-approved agreements or a reviewed production policy.
